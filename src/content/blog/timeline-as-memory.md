Timelines are usually treated like proof: roles, dates, milestones, receipts. They can do that job, but they can also do something warmer.

A useful timeline can hold the shape of attention over time. It can show when a project started, when a practice changed, when a season taught you something, or when a question became impossible to ignore.

## A Living List

The timeline on this site is intentionally data-driven. Adding a new item should be boring in the best way: open one file, add the date, title, category, description, and optional link.

The easier it is to maintain, the more likely it is to stay honest.
